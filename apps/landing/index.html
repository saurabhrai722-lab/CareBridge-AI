Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CareBridge AI integrate with existing hospital EHR systems? ↓

CareBridge AI connects natively via standardized HL7 FHIR R4 interfaces, supporting automated bundle ingestion, MedicationStatement generation, and ABDM health ID validation without altering your existing clinical database schema.

What happens if the primary health center has no active internet connection? ↓

Our Android mobile client utilizes an offline-first architecture powered by Drift and SQLite. Field nurses can register intake, update patient vitals, and complete appointments completely offline. Cryptographic peer sync executes seamlessly as soon as cellular data or Wi-Fi reconnects.

Is patient identity reconciliation fully automated by machine learning? ↓

No. By clinical safety design, CareBridge enforces a strict human-in-the-loop policy. While our model scores candidate matches with confidence indices, a human clinician or medical record specialist must explicitly review discrepancies and click 'Confirm & Link' before any medical record linkage occurs.

How are 30-day readmission risk factors explained to attending physicians? ↓

Alongside each probability score, the platform renders a calibrated SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) visual contribution bar chart. This clearly shows physicians which factors (e.g., polypharmacy, recent emergency admissions, elevated blood pressure) contributed positively to risk, and which protective behaviors mitigated it.
